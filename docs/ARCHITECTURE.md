# Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL)

# Architecture

Version 1.0

---

# Introduction

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project dedicated to the deterministic study of Ethereum protocol composition.

Rather than implementing individual Ethereum standards, OECL studies how independent protocol layers compose, interact, preserve deterministic behavior, and generate reproducible scientific evidence.

The architecture is intentionally designed to separate open research infrastructure from higher-order research capabilities while preserving modularity, reproducibility, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Project Identity

OECL maintains its own scientific mission, governance principles, technical direction, and architectural identity.

The project is currently maintained by **tenio.eth**.

References to tenio.eth identify the current maintainer of the project and, where applicable, the copyright holder of original works created by tenio.eth.

The scientific mission, architectural principles, and long-term direction of OECL are intentionally independent of any specific organization, commercial product, or future maintainership.

---

# Architectural Philosophy

OECL follows a layered architecture.

Each engine performs a clearly defined responsibility.

Research capabilities emerge through the composition of independent research engines rather than monolithic implementations.

Each architectural layer is intentionally designed to remain modular, reproducible, independently verifiable, and capable of evolving without compromising the integrity of the overall research process.

---

# Layer 1 — Experimental Infrastructure

The first layer executes deterministic protocol experiments.

Its responsibilities include:

* protocol execution
* scenario execution
* validation
* dataset generation
* benchmark generation

This layer produces reproducible experimental evidence.

---

# Layer 2 — Analytical Infrastructure

The analytical layer transforms raw experimental evidence into structured observations.

Examples include:

* protocol requirements
* protocol relationships
* composition matrix
* emergent properties
* protocol intelligence

This layer identifies measurable characteristics of protocol composition.

---

# Layer 3 — Scientific Reasoning

The scientific layer transforms structured observations into research hypotheses.

Capabilities include:

* hypothesis generation
* hypothesis validation
* automatic scenario generation
* adaptive experimentation

Rather than executing experiments directly, this layer determines what should be investigated next based on accumulated evidence.

---

# Layer 4 — Knowledge Accumulation

The highest layer accumulates long-term scientific knowledge.

Its objectives include:

* research memory
* accumulated observations
* validated hypotheses
* long-term protocol knowledge

Knowledge is intended to grow continuously as additional experiments are executed.

---

# OECL Core

OECL Core represents the public scientific foundation of the OECL ecosystem.

Its objective is to provide open, deterministic, and reproducible research infrastructure for studying Ethereum protocol composition.

Core capabilities include:

* Experiment Engine
* Scenario Engine
* Benchmark Engine
* Requirements Engine
* Pattern Discovery
* Relationship Engine
* Composition Matrix
* Validation
* Dataset Formats
* Documentation

OECL Core is intended to remain an open scientific infrastructure that can be independently adopted, extended, validated, and reused by the Ethereum ecosystem.

---

# OECL Research

OECL Research extends the Core without replacing it.

Its objective is to transform deterministic experimental evidence into accumulated scientific knowledge through progressively higher-order research capabilities.

Examples include:

* Composition Intelligence
* Research Memory
* Research Knowledge
* Adaptive Research
* Hypothesis Generation
* Hypothesis Validation
* Automatic Scenario Generation
* Future AI-assisted research systems

OECL Research builds upon the open infrastructure provided by OECL Core while preserving interoperability between layers.

# Summary

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project whose objective is to continuously generate, validate, preserve, and expand scientific knowledge about deterministic Ethereum protocol composition through reproducible experimentation.

Its modular architecture provides an open scientific foundation capable of supporting long-term research, advanced analytical capabilities, and future ecosystem evolution while preserving reproducibility, interoperability, and architectural consistency.
