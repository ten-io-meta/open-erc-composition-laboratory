# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Version 1.0 (Draft)

---

# Purpose

This document describes the intended Contributor License Agreement (CLA) policy for the Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL).

The purpose of the CLA is to establish a transparent legal framework for future external contributions while preserving both contributor ownership and the long-term sustainability of the OECL ecosystem.

This document complements the project's:

* LICENSE
* CONTRIBUTING.md
* IP_POLICY.md
* GOVERNANCE.md

---

# Project Identity

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project dedicated to deterministic Ethereum protocol composition.

OECL is currently maintained by **tenio.eth**.

References to tenio.eth identify the current maintainer of the project and, where applicable, the copyright holder of original works created by tenio.eth.

The Contributor License Agreement exists to support the long-term evolution of the OECL project regardless of future organizational or maintainership changes.

---

# Why a CLA Exists

As the project grows, external contributions may become an important part of the OECL ecosystem.

Without a Contributor License Agreement, long-term project maintenance may become more complex due to fragmented ownership of accepted contributions.

The CLA is intended to provide legal clarity for both contributors and project maintainers.

---

# Contributor Ownership

Contributors always retain the copyright to their original work.

Submitting a contribution to OECL does not transfer copyright ownership.

The CLA is intended to grant additional usage rights without changing ownership.

---

# License Granted to tenio.eth

For accepted contributions, contributors may be asked to grant tenio.eth a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free license to:

* use;
* reproduce;
* modify;
* adapt;
* distribute;
* publish;
* sublicense;
* relicense;
* integrate;
* commercially use

their accepted contributions as part of the OECL ecosystem.

This license is intended to ensure that accepted contributions can continue to be maintained, improved, redistributed, and incorporated into future OECL initiatives.

---

# Scope

The intended license applies only to contributions that are voluntarily submitted and accepted into the project.

Rejected contributions remain entirely under the ownership of their original authors.

---

# Future OECL Ecosystem

The Contributor License Agreement is intended to support the long-term evolution of the OECL ecosystem, including but not limited to:

* OECL Core
* OECL Research
* OECL Enterprise
* OECL Cloud
* hosted services
* educational material
* scientific publications
* technical documentation

The CLA helps ensure that future development is not restricted by fragmented ownership of accepted contributions.

---

# Attribution

OECL values proper attribution.

Accepted contributors should receive appropriate recognition whenever practical.

Nothing in the CLA is intended to remove contributor attribution.

---

# Future Adoption

At the time of publication, the Contributor License Agreement is provided as a draft policy.

The official CLA will be adopted before external contributions are routinely accepted into the project.

Once adopted, contributors may be required to explicitly accept the CLA before their contributions are merged.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Contributor License Agreement is intended to balance two complementary objectives:

* preserving contributor ownership;
* ensuring the long-term sustainability, maintainability, interoperability, and evolution of the Open ERC Composition Laboratory.

The CLA exists to support an open scientific ecosystem capable of evolving over time while providing legal clarity for contributors and project maintainers.
