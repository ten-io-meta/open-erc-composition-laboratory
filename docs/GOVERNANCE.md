# Purpose

This document defines the governance principles of the Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL).

Its purpose is to establish the long-term scientific, technical, and organizational principles that guide the evolution of the project.

Governance exists to preserve the identity, reproducibility, architectural consistency, and scientific integrity of OECL independently of future organizational or technological evolution.

---

# Project Identity

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project dedicated to deterministic Ethereum protocol composition.

OECL maintains its own scientific mission, governance principles, technical direction, and architectural identity.

The project is currently maintained by **tenio.eth**.

References to tenio.eth identify the current maintainer of the project and, where applicable, the copyright holder of original works created by tenio.eth.

The governance principles of OECL are intentionally designed to remain independent of any specific organization, commercial product, or future maintainership.

---

# Mission

The mission of OECL is to provide an open scientific infrastructure for reproducible experimentation, deterministic validation, and long-term research into Ethereum protocol composition.

Rather than implementing individual Ethereum standards, OECL studies how independent protocol layers compose, interact, preserve deterministic behavior, and generate reproducible scientific evidence.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision of OECL is to become a trusted research infrastructure for deterministic Ethereum protocol composition.

Knowledge generated through experimentation should accumulate over time, remain reproducible, and contribute to a growing body of scientific understanding about protocol interoperability.

---

# Governance Principles

## 1. Reproducible Research

Research should be reproducible whenever possible.

Scientific conclusions should originate from observable experimental evidence.

Reproducibility is considered a fundamental characteristic of the project.

---

## 2. Protocol Neutrality

OECL does not promote or prioritize individual Ethereum standards.

The project studies protocol composition objectively regardless of protocol origin, authorship, or implementation.

---

## 3. Deterministic Validation

Deterministic experiments should be preferred whenever applicable.

Observable evidence should always take precedence over assumptions or theoretical expectations.

---

## 4. Incremental Scientific Knowledge

Knowledge within OECL is accumulated through:

* experiments;
* datasets;
* benchmarks;
* protocol relationships;
* emergent observations;
* hypotheses;
* validation campaigns;
* accumulated research memory.

Every research campaign should contribute additional scientific knowledge.

---

## 5. Modular Architecture

Every engine within OECL should have a clearly defined responsibility.

Higher-order research capabilities should emerge through the composition of independent engines rather than monolithic implementations.

Architectural consistency is considered essential for long-term sustainability.

---

## 6. Open Scientific Collaboration

OECL encourages open collaboration among researchers, developers, protocol authors, auditors, and members of the Ethereum ecosystem.

Contributions should strengthen reproducibility, scientific quality, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Project Architecture

OECL is intentionally organized into complementary architectural layers.

## OECL Core

OECL Core represents the public scientific foundation of the project.

Its responsibilities include:

* deterministic experiment execution;
* scenario execution;
* benchmark generation;
* protocol requirements;
* relationship analysis;
* composition matrix generation;
* dataset generation;
* validation;
* documentation.

OECL Core exists to provide open and reproducible scientific infrastructure for Ethereum protocol composition.

---

## OECL Research

OECL Research extends the Core through progressively higher-order research capabilities.

Examples include:

* Composition Intelligence;
* Research Memory;
* Research Knowledge;
* Hypothesis Generation;
* Hypothesis Validation;
* Adaptive Research Planning;
* Automatic Scenario Generation;
* future AI-assisted research systems.

OECL Research builds upon the Core while preserving interoperability and reproducibility.

---

## Future Ecosystem

The intended long-term evolution of the OECL ecosystem is:

```text
OECL

↓

OECL Core

↓

OECL Research

↓

OECL Enterprise

↓

OECL Cloud
```

Each layer extends the previous one while preserving interoperability, reproducibility, and architectural consistency.

This architectural vision does not imply that every layer currently exists or shares identical licensing terms.

* scientific integrity;
* architectural consistency;
* documentation quality;
* reproducibility;
