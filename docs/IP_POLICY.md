# Purpose

This document defines the intellectual property principles governing the Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL).

Its objective is to provide long-term clarity regarding ownership, contributor rights, research assets, licensing strategy, and the future evolution of the OECL ecosystem.

This policy complements, but does not replace, the project's:

* LICENSE
* LICENSING.md
* GOVERNANCE.md
* CONTRIBUTING.md
* CLA.md

---

# Project Identity

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project dedicated to deterministic Ethereum protocol composition.

OECL maintains its own scientific mission, governance principles, technical direction, and architectural identity.

The project is currently maintained by **tenio.eth**.

References to tenio.eth identify the current maintainer of the project and, where applicable, the copyright holder of original works created by tenio.eth.

The intellectual property principles described in this document are intended to support the long-term continuity of the OECL ecosystem independently of future organizational or maintainership changes.

---

# Copyright

Unless otherwise stated, the copyright to original works remains with their respective authors.

Original works include, but are not limited to:

* source code;
* documentation;
* datasets;
* research methodologies;
* architectural designs;
* experimental reports;
* publications;
* original research materials.

Copyright ownership and software licensing are independent legal concepts.

---

# Ownership

Ownership of original work remains with its original author unless explicitly transferred through a separate written agreement.

Nothing in this policy transfers copyright ownership from contributors to tenio.eth.

Contributor ownership is preserved throughout the lifecycle of the project.

---

# Contributor Licensing

Accepted contributions may become subject to the Contributor License Agreement (CLA) adopted by the project.

The purpose of the CLA is to grant the project the rights necessary to maintain, improve, distribute, sublicense, evolve, and, where applicable, commercially use accepted contributions while preserving contributor ownership.

The complete Contributor License Agreement is documented separately in:

* `docs/CLA.md`

---

# OECL Core

OECL Core represents the public scientific foundation of the project.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Core components are intended to remain openly licensed to encourage:

* reproducible research;
* scientific collaboration;
* protocol interoperability;
* deterministic experimentation;
* ecosystem adoption.

---

# OECL Research

OECL Research represents advanced research capabilities built upon the Core infrastructure.

Research components may be distributed under licensing terms different from those of the Core, including commercial licensing where appropriate.

This separation exists to support both open scientific collaboration and the long-term sustainability of the OECL ecosystem.

---

# Research Assets

The OECL ecosystem may contain research assets including:

* source code;
* software architecture;
* datasets;
* benchmark results;
* experimental methodologies;
* protocol intelligence;
* composition intelligence;
* hypothesis models;
* validation campaigns;
* research memory;
* accumulated knowledge;
* documentation;
* scientific publications.

Different categories of research assets may be distributed under different licensing terms depending on their intended purpose.

---

# Attribution

OECL values proper attribution.

Contributors should receive appropriate recognition whenever practical.

Scientific publications should appropriately acknowledge contributors whose work materially supports published research.

Attribution should never be interpreted as transferring ownership.

---

# Future Evolution

The intellectual property strategy of OECL is intentionally designed to support a layered ecosystem.

The expected long-term structure is:

```text
OECL

↓

OECL Core

↓

OECL Research

↓

OECL Enterprise

↓

OECL Cloud
```

Each layer may adopt licensing terms appropriate to its objectives while preserving interoperability with the Core infrastructure.

The existence of this architectural vision does not imply that every layer currently exists or shares identical licensing terms.

---

# Relationship Between Governance, Licensing and Intellectual Property

Within the OECL ecosystem these concepts remain intentionally independent.

* Governance defines how the project is managed.
* Licensing defines how software may be used.
* Intellectual Property defines ownership and contributor rights.
* The Contributor License Agreement defines the rights granted for accepted contributions.

Maintaining this separation improves long-term legal clarity and project sustainability.

---

# Guiding Principle

OECL is founded on the belief that open scientific infrastructure and sustainable long-term research are complementary objectives.

The project seeks to maximize scientific collaboration while preserving the ability to responsibly maintain, evolve, extend, and sustain the OECL ecosystem over time.

This Intellectual Property Policy reflects those principles.
