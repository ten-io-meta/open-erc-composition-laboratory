# Licensing Strategy

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECL) is an independent open research project dedicated to deterministic Ethereum protocol composition.

Its licensing strategy is designed to balance open scientific collaboration with the long-term sustainability of the project and its future ecosystem.

This document describes the intended licensing model for the OECL ecosystem.

---

# Project Identity

OECL is an independent research project.

The project is currently maintained by **tenio.eth**.

References to tenio.eth identify the current maintainer of the project and, where applicable, the copyright holder of original works created by tenio.eth.

The licensing strategy described in this document applies to the OECL ecosystem independently of its current maintainer.

---

# OECL Core

OECL Core represents the public research infrastructure of the project.

Its objectives include:

* reproducible experimentation
* deterministic validation
* protocol interoperability
* benchmark generation
* protocol requirements
* relationship analysis
* composition research
* reference implementations

Examples include:

* Experiment Engine
* Scenario Engine
* Benchmark Engine
* Requirements Engine
* Pattern Discovery
* Relationship Engine
* Composition Matrix
* Validation
* Dataset Formats
* Documentation

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, OECL Core is intended to remain openly licensed to encourage ecosystem collaboration and reproducible scientific research.

---

# OECL Research

OECL Research represents the advanced research platform built upon the Core infrastructure.

Examples include:

* Composition Intelligence
* Hypothesis Generation
* Hypothesis Validation
* Adaptive Research Planning
* Automatic Scenario Generation
* Research Memory
* Research Knowledge
* Future AI-assisted research systems

Research components may be distributed under licensing terms different from the Core, including commercial licensing where appropriate.

# Licensing Principles

OECL follows the following licensing principles.

* Open scientific collaboration.
* Reproducible research.
* Transparent protocol validation.
* Modular architecture.
* Sustainable long-term development.
* Licensing flexibility for future ecosystem components.

# Long-Term Vision

The objective of OECL is to establish an enduring open research infrastructure for Ethereum protocol composition while enabling advanced research platforms, enterprise tooling, and hosted services to evolve around that infrastructure under appropriate licensing models.
