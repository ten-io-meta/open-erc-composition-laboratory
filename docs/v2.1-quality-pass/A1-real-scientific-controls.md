## Purpose

The V2.1 control suite exists to demonstrate that the generic OECL composition pipeline does not prefer a desired scientific outcome.

The same evidence architecture must be capable of producing:

- SUPPORT when sufficient positive evidence exists;
- CHALLENGE when an observed boundary is violated;
- INCONCLUSIVE when the available evidence is insufficient.

The controls are fixed before any V2.1 motor modification intended to satisfy them.

---

## Scientific rule

OECL does not choose a verdict and search for evidence afterward.

Evidence enters first.

The scientific state follows.

---

## SUPPORTED control contract

A real composition can act as a positive control only if the generic pipeline establishes all required layers:

1. a qualifying composition candidate exists;
2. relevant participant boundaries are known;
3. relevant boundaries are observed;
4. no required boundary is observed violated;
5. compatibility evidence is sufficient for a firm decision;
6. a functional composition configuration is evidenced.

Documentary relation alone is insufficient.

Lexical similarity is insufficient.

Coexistence is insufficient.

Zero known boundaries is insufficient.

---

## Positive-control candidate — ERC-8004 × ERC-8060

ERC-8004 × ERC-8060 is preregistered as the first positive-control candidate because an older pair-specific control already established:

- 17 known constraints;
- 17 preserved;

This does not pre-register a SUPPORTED global-composition verdict.

The pair becomes the V2.1 positive control only if the generic N-protocol architecture independently reaches the SUPPORTED contract.

If the generic evidence remains insufficient, OECL must retain the actual result instead of forcing SUPPORT.

---

## CHALLENGED control contract

A challenged control requires:

1. a qualifying composition candidate;
2. a known relevant boundary;
3. an observation of that boundary under the proposed composition;
4. exact evidence that the boundary is violated.

The following are not evidence of CHALLENGE:

- no candidate discovered;
- no The Graph attribution;
- missing evidence;
- unresolved needs;
- lack of known boundaries.

The challenged pair remains intentionally unselected at A1.

Selection will happen only after a real evidence-backed violation candidate is identified.
