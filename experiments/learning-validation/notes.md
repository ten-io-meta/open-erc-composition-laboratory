# Learning Validation Experiment

## Objective

Verify whether OECL V1 updates its derived research knowledge after introducing additional experimental evidence.

## Procedure

Five additional valid adaptive scenarios were introduced into the research pipeline.

The complete OECL pipeline was executed again.

## Results

Scenarios:

38 → 43

Passed:

22 → 27

Success rate:

57.89% → 62.79%

Compatibility:

58% → 63%

Research memory:

Updated

Protocol intelligence:

Updated

Research hypotheses:

Updated

Research report:

Updated

## Conclusion

OECL V1 successfully propagated new experimental evidence across every research stage.

The benchmark, relationships, matrix, protocol intelligence, hypotheses, validation plans, research memory and final research report were automatically updated without modifying the research engines.

This experiment demonstrates that OECL V1 behaves as a deterministic research system capable of incorporating new evidence into its accumulated knowledge.
