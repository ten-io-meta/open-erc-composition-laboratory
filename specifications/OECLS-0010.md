# OECLS-0010

# Governance Specification

Status: Draft

Version: 0.1

---

# Title

Governance Specification

---

# Purpose

This specification defines how the Open ERC Composition Laboratory (OECLS) evolves over time while preserving openness, reproducibility and scientific integrity.

The objective of governance is not to control research outcomes, but to ensure that the methodology remains transparent, extensible and community-driven.

---

# Principles

The laboratory is governed by the following principles:

- openness
- transparency
- reproducibility
- protocol neutrality
- evidence-based evolution

---

# Open Participation

Anyone may:

- propose new specifications
- contribute benchmarks
- submit protocol adapters
- publish datasets
- report issues
- suggest improvements

Participation is independent of protocol ownership.

---

# Specification Process

Every OECLS specification follows the same lifecycle.

Possible states:

- Draft
- Review
- Accepted
- Final
- Deprecated

Specifications remain permanently accessible.

---

# Backward Compatibility

Whenever possible, new specifications should remain compatible with previous versions.

Breaking changes should create new specification versions rather than modifying existing ones.

---

# Scientific Neutrality

The laboratory does not endorse protocols.

Experiments are designed to observe behavior, not to validate predefined conclusions.

Evidence always takes precedence over assumptions.

---

# Reproducibility

Published experiments should include sufficient information for independent verification.

Independent reproduction is encouraged.

---

# Open Data

Experimental datasets should remain publicly accessible whenever possible.

Results should reference the experiment and specification versions used.

---

# Community Contributions

Contributors may submit:

- protocol adapters
- benchmarks
- experiments
- metrics
- datasets
- documentation

All contributions should follow the relevant OECLS specifications.

---

# Future Evolution

Future specifications may extend the laboratory while preserving the core principles defined in OECLS-0001.

---

# Summary

The Governance Specification defines the principles that allow the Open ERC Composition Laboratory to evolve as an open, reproducible and community-driven research infrastructure.
